# Help

Lists what the agent can do, from the help line in each skill's front matter.

## Examples
- help
- what can you do
- what are your commands
- how does this work
- show me what you can do
- options
- I'm lost
- what do you know
- help me
- list your commands
- what can I ask you
- how do I use you
- what are you able to do
- any tips
- commands
- what can I say
- how do I use {you|this|this thing|ptilu}{?|}
- {explain|show me} how {to use you|this works}
- {give me|I need} {some |}{help|guidance|instructions}
- {list|tell me} your {skills|capabilities|features}
- what {should|could} I {type|say|try}{?|}
- {instructions|the manual|usage}{ please|}
- {I'm stuck|I don't know what to do|where do I start}

## Replies
- say: I can {list}.
- try: Try “make a folder called drafts in documents”, “remind me to call mum next friday at 3pm”, “weather tomorrow” or “I live in Munich”.
