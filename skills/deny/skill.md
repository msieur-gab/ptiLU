# No

A no with nothing to cancel. No after a question cancels it.

## Examples
- no
- nope
- cancel
- never mind
- nevermind
- stop
- forget it
- don't
- no thanks
- abort
- no way
- not now
- cancel that
- leave it
- n
- scratch that
- actually no
- drop it
- no no
- not really
- I'd rather not
- hold on
- wait no
- skip it
- don't do that
- no, leave it
- nah, cancel

## Replies
- say: OK. There's nothing to cancel.
