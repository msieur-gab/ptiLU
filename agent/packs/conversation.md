# Conversation

`contact:` above is the address the contact form sends to (empty: the form says so instead of sending).

How people say yes and no, the words that carry no request, and what the agent says in its own name.
One line can hold several words, separated by commas.

## Yes
- yes, yeah, yep, yup, sure, ok, okay, go ahead, do it, please do, correct, right, exactly, of course, that's it

## No
- no, nope, nah, cancel, don't, do not, never mind, not now, stop, wrong, not that, neither

## Fillers
Taken off the start or the end of a sentence, never from the middle.
- please, can you, could you, would you, will you, can you please, could you please, i want you to
- i'd like you to, just, quickly, for me, now please, so, ok so

## Small words
Words that say nothing about which skill is meant.
- the, a, an, to, me, my, i, i'm, it, it's, is, are, be, of, for, in, on, at, and, or, this, that
- you, your, can, could, would, will, please, now, just, some, any, do, does, did, what, what's
- with, from, about, there, here, up, so, then, also, us, we, our, mine, again, one, by, as
- which, who, whom, whose, how, why, when, where, much, many, should, must, may, might, shall, was, were, been
- have, has, had, he, she, his, her, they, them, their, these, those, if, but, not, very, too, more, most, than
- tell, explain, describe, know, think, want, like, need, said, say, get, make, really, actually, even, still

## More
- more, tell me more, go on, continue, and then, what else, keep going, anything else, more please, and

## Replies
- cant: I can't do that yet. | That's not something I can do yet.
- not sure: Do you mean: {label}?
- do you mean: I'm not sure which you mean. One of these?
- ok no: OK, I won't. | Alright, never mind.
- not written: I haven't published work on this yet. Send me the question and I'll answer you myself.
- ok: OK. | Alright.
- far: That's outside what I work on, so I'd rather not guess. | I don't have anything on that, and it's not my field.
- several: {n} pieces of my work answer this. | {n} of my projects are about this.
- no more: That's all I have written about {title}.
