# Conversation

What every pack shares: how people say yes and no, the words that carry no request, and what the
engine says in its own name. One line can hold several words, separated by commas.

## Yes
- yes, yeah, yep, yup, sure, ok, okay, go ahead, do it, please do, correct, right, exactly, of course

## No
- no, nope, nah, cancel, don't, do not, never mind, not now, stop, wrong

## Fillers
Taken off the start or the end of a sentence, never from the middle.
- please, can you, could you, would you, will you, can you please, could you please, i want you to
- i'd like you to, just, quickly, for me, now please, so, ok so

## Small words
Words that say nothing about which skill is meant: the taught-words check doesn't count them.
- the, a, an, to, me, my, i, i'm, it, it's, is, are, be, of, for, in, on, at, and, or, this, that
- you, your, can, could, would, will, please, now, just, some, any, do, does, did, what, what's
- with, from, about, there, here, up, so, then, also, us, we, our, mine, again, one, by, as
- which, who, whom, whose, how, why, when, where, much, many, should, must, may, might, shall, was, were, been
- have, has, had, he, she, his, her, they, them, their, these, those, if, but, not, very, too, more, most, than
- tell, explain, describe, know, think, want, like, need, said, say

## Follow-up
A sentence starting with these may complete the request before it.
- and, and what about, what about, how about, and for, same for, also, and also, then

## More
- more, tell me more, go on, continue, and then, what else, keep going, anything else, more please

## Question words
A sentence starting with one of these is a question, and may be answered from the content.
- what, what's, who, who's, why, how, when, where, which, is, are, does, do, did, tell, explain, describe

## Chips
- yes
- no

## Replies
- cant: I can't do that yet. | That's not something I can do yet.
- not sure: Do you want me to {label}?
- unknown word: I don't know {word} yet. Do you want me to {label}?
- ok no: OK, I won't. | Alright, never mind.
- which one: {a}, or {b}?
- do you mean: I'm not sure I have that. Do you mean one of these?
- nothing written: I don't have anything written about that. | Nothing I've been given covers that.
- no more: That's all I have written about {title}.
- me name: You're {value}.
- me place: You live in {value}.
