Where home is. Kept on this device only, with the sentence you said it in.

## People say
- i live in {place}
- (remember (that)) i live in {place}
- my home is (in) {place}
- home is {place}
- i'm living in {place}
- i've moved to {place}

## Also said
- I'm based in Munich
- I stay in Lyon these days
- my town is Kyoto

## What it needs
- {place}: a place. If it's missing, ask: Where do you live?

## Samples
- place: Munich | Paris | Kyoto | Lisbon | Shenzhen

## Does
memory.set place {place}

## Replies
- done: OK, home is {place}. | Noted: you live in {place}.
- refused: I didn't catch the place.
