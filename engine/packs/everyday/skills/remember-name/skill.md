What to call you. Kept on this device only, with the sentence you said it in.

## People say
- (you can) call me {name}
- my name is {name}
- my name's {name}
- i'm called {name}
- (remember (that)) my name is {name}
- (please) call me {name} from now on

## Also said
- I'd like you to call me Sam
- the name is Eva
- you can address me as Gab

## What it needs
- {name}: a name. If it's missing, ask: What should I call you?

## Samples
- name: Gab | Gabriel | Eva | Romain | Léa | Sam | Yuki | Amara | Lucas | Mia | Noah | Chloé | Tom | Ines | Doc | Boss | Chief | Professor | Kiki

## Does
memory.set name {name}

## Replies
- done: Nice to meet you, {name}. | OK, I'll call you {name}.
- refused: I didn't catch your name.
