## Invite
What it says to a question for the author, or when nothing matches. Gab's own drafts, word for word (my-agent
docs/questions.md, "When nothing matches"), one picked at random.
- I haven't built this one yet, but I have a view on it. Send me the question and I'll answer you myself.
- Nothing in my work covers this yet. That doesn't mean I can't help. Tell me more?
- That's a question I'd rather answer properly than guess at. Send it to me.
- I haven't worked on exactly this. I'd like to hear why you're asking.
- No project to show for that one, but I do have thoughts. Want them?
- You've found a gap in my work. Send me the question and I'll answer it properly.
- I'd be guessing if I showed you something now. Send it over and get a real answer.
- Not something I've built yet. That's often where the interesting work starts.
- I don't have a case study for this. I do have an opinion. Ask me directly.
- That one's new here. Leave it with me and I'll reply personally.
