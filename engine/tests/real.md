# Real sentences

Sentences Gab typed on the phone (20 November 2026), with what should happen. `node engine/test.js`
replays them by words; `node engine/meaning/check.mjs` by words then meaning. A line is
`- sentence → file` (answered from that file), `→ not file` (anything but that file), or `→ nothing`.

- Our compliance tool is hosted in the US. Is that a problem? → autoflow
- we are building a due diligence platform but all users complain that is very hard to organize the fire. how will you approach such a problem? → dowgo
- we need help to design a due diligence plate-forme → dowgo
- we are building a jewel diligence platform and research for designer. are you working on that → not autoflow
- how much data privacy is influencing your design process? → not autoflow
- Can a product grow without advertising? → vrooom
- What is designing by abstention? → designing-by-abstention
